What the ladder represents

Small, useful steps—not one all-or-nothing retirement number.

  • Cash milestones3–6 months of essential costs. Age does not affect these.
  • Coast independenceThe amount you need invested today so that, without contributing any more, it can grow enough to fund your retirement by your target age.
  • Financial independenceThe amount invested to cover your retirement spending.
Age changes your projection and Coast independence. Spending and withdrawal rate change these targets.
